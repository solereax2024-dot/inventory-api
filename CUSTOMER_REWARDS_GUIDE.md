## ❓ FAQs

### Q: Do I need an account to shop?
**A:** No! Just browse normally. To earn points, just set a username.

### Q: Is my username private?
**A:** Yes! It's only visible to you unless you share it for referrals.

### Q: Can I change my username?
**A:** Currently, usernames are permanent. Choose wisely!

### Q: What if I forget my username?
**A:** It's saved in your browser. Check: Settings → Cookies/Storage

### Q: Do points ever expire?
**A:** No! Your points never expire. Use them anytime.

### Q: How long until badges unlock?
**A:** Instantly! When you reach the requirement, badge unlocks immediately.

### Q: Can I see other players' profiles?
**A:** You can see the leaderboard with top 10 players and their point counts.

### Q: What if I don't set a username?
**A:** You can still shop normally! Just won't earn rewards. Set one anytime!

### Q: How do I redeem points for discount?
**A:** During checkout, there's a "Redeem Points" section. Enter amount and click redeem!
